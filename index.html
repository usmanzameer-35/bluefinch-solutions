Initial Feasibility of Medical Billing

A high-level roadmap for making the New Jersey company client-ready first, confirming two to three clients, and then activating Bluefinch Solutions in Pakistan within a targeted 10 working days.

01
Operating model

New Jersey leads commercially. Bluefinch Solutions delivers.

The companies remain separate and work through documented responsibilities, contracts and readiness gates.

New Jersey company

  • Confirm US and New Jersey requirements.
  • Become fully active and client-ready.
  • Prepare contracts, compliance and marketing material.
  • Secure approximately two to three clients.
  • Own client communication and relationships.

Bluefinch Solutions Pakistan

  • Confirm remaining Pakistan requirements.
  • Prepare secure systems and controls.
  • Recruit after client confirmation.
  • Train and supervise the delivery team.
  • Deliver the agreed scope and report performance.
02
Before operations

Four prerequisites control the launch

No production work starts until each gate has clear evidence and joint approval.

Gate 1

NJ ready

Legal, regulatory, banking, insurance, contract, security and marketing matters are confirmed.

Gate 2

Clients ready

Two to three clients, or enough workload, are confirmed with signed scope.

Gate 3

Bluefinch ready

People, systems, training, policies and registrations match the workload.

Gate 4

Launch approved

Both teams approve contracts, access, testing and escalation.

No fixed planning costs are included. Each team must confirm current requirements, actual costs and realistic completion dates.
03
Guiding checklist only

New Jersey company prerequisites

These items are a starting point—not a definitive legal checklist. Every applicable requirement must be independently confirmed in New Jersey.

Business and compliance

  • Activate the business and confirm good standing.
  • Confirm tax, reporting and employer registrations.
  • Confirm third-party billing or other authorisation.
  • Establish banking, accounting and approval controls.
  • Confirm insurance, healthcare, employment and tax obligations.

Documents and market readiness

  • Prepare MSA, SOW, BAA and offshore approval.
  • Confirm HIPAA, payer and EHR requirements.
  • Prepare security, incident and service-level documents.
  • Prepare profile, proposals, pricing approach and CRM.
  • Use references and controlled outreach to secure clients.
NJ action: return with confirmed requirements, evidence, professional guidance, actual costs and completion dates.
04
Delivery readiness

Bluefinch Solutions starts from an established base

The Pakistan operation already has most core business and physical infrastructure.

Already available

  • Registered sole-proprietor business.
  • Business bank account, NTN and current return filing.
  • Office with seats for three employees and one manager.
  • Internet, VoIP phones, printer and scanner.
  • Existing company website.

Still to complete or confirm

  • IP whitelisting and secure connectivity.
  • PSEB registration or applicable requirement.
  • Remaining tax, labour, export and regulatory matters.
  • HIPAA training and PHI procedures.
  • MFA, managed devices and endpoint protection.
  • QA, reporting, continuity and incident response.

Client confirmation unlocks recruitment

Bluefinch Solutions begins hiring only after approximately two to three clients—or enough contracted work—are confirmed.

06
Conditional target

Operations can target launch within 10 working days

The clock starts after client confirmation and depends on timely access, recruitment, documents, training and security approval.

Activation work

  • Recruit the small team needed for signed volume.
  • Complete IP whitelisting, accounts and MFA.
  • Complete workflow, security and HIPAA training.
  • Approve SOPs, QA, reports and escalation.
  • Test with synthetic or approved test data.

Launch condition

Begin controlled delivery only when both teams approve readiness. The target does not override client access, licensing or compliance dependencies.

07
Shared financial view

Each team confirms its own costs and timelines

The report sets direction. Detailed research belongs to the responsible team.

Each team records

  • Completed and outstanding items.
  • Named task owner.
  • Required official confirmation.
  • Expected completion date.
  • Actual current cost or quotation.
  • Dependencies, risks and evidence.

Then both teams share

The combined schedules will show what must be paid, when money is needed and when operations can realistically begin. No estimated planning costs are added before findings and quotations are available.
